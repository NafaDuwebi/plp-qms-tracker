+
Audit finding
CRM has not been consistently updated by the BDM following interview activity, resulting in a lack of recorded evidence across three process steps.
Nathan Spooner
Agreed corrective action
BDM to update CRM for recent interview activity where possible and implement a consistent practice of recording all interview scheduling and feedback going forward.
■ QMS Reference — Process Architecture
Step 5.5.1 Upon acceptance of CV(s), BDM or Client Lead liaises with the enquirer and Consultant to schedule an interview (if required). This is recorded and considered in the CRM.

Step 5.6.2 If Consultant is unsuccessful, BDM or Client Lead discusses feedback with Consultant and Head of Delivery. Feedback recorded on the CRM. Head of Delivery reverts to step 5.1 if required.
ISO clause 8.3 Design and development of products and services
Evidence needed CRM records showing: interview scheduled and date noted; outcome of interview recorded; feedback discussed with consultant and Head of Delivery captured.
